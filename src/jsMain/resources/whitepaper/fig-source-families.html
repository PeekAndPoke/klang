8 voices spread over 0.20 semitones. The outermost run about 1.3 Hz away from the note at 220 Hz, so they slip a full cycle against it every 0.8 s: that slow beating is the movement in the sound.

The detune math

Voice n of v is sign(x) × |x|1.2 × spread / 2 semitones from the note, with x = 2n / (v - 1) - 1. The gain-weighted mean offset is taken out, so the pitch of the whole stack stays on the note. Gains run from 1 at the centre to 0.9 at the edges, then each voice gets a random ±15% (±6% for the centre voice), and the stack is scaled to sum to 1. Start phases are random.
