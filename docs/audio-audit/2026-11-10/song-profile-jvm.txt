HOW TO READ
  "Inside renderBlock": the stack holds KlangAudioRenderer.renderBlock (the timed part of the benchmark), or the stack is
  truncated at JFR's 64 frames (deep Ignitor trees) and holds engine frames and no census frame. Everything else on the
  thread is the harness: compile, pattern query, scheduling the events, and the per-block census (GraphCensus and its
  string key), which runs between the timed blocks.
  Self time: the leaf frame of the sample, inlined frames counted as their own method (JFR keeps them).
  Areas: each sample is given to ONE area by walking from the leaf towards main, skipping shared cores (utils, filters,
  effects, DistortionCore, EnvelopeCore, CrushCore, Oversampler, KnobGlide, java, kotlin) until a frame decides:
    build of voices and chains  any frame of VoiceFactory, IgnitorDslRuntime (buildRaw/buildIgnitor/buildExciter),
                                IgnitorDslOptimizer, KatalystChainBuilder, IgnitorRegistry.optimized (checked first)
    scheduling                  VoiceScheduler, SampleStore, the dispatcher's command handling
    Katalyst stages             katalyst.* (the cylinder chains: reverb, delay, body, compressor, ...)
    sources                     the oscillator, noise, pluck and sample Ignitors
    per-note stages             every other Ignitor (filters, envelopes, shapers, eq, regions, memo and arithmetic
                                plumbing) and the Voice's own stages
    mixing                      voice_channel (mixIntoCylinder, peak), cylinders, the house stage, the master
    engine loop                 the renderer, engine and dispatcher's own frames
  So the reverb's Reverb.process counts as a Katalyst stage when a cylinder chain runs it, the guitar rig's EqCore
  as a per-note stage (its EqIgnitor), the house limiter's Compressor as mixing.
  GC: not in the execution samples (GC threads); its pauses are counted from the jdk.GarbageCollection events.
  Allocation: JFR's ObjectAllocationSample, a THROTTLED sample (1,386 events over 90 s, about 2 MB of allocation per
  event): the totals are estimates, and a steady rate under about 10 KB per second of audio (about 30 B per block) can
  have no event at all. The steady per-block allocation of single stages is the stage cost table's job (ThreadMXBean,
  stage-cost.txt: no row allocates on the JVM).
