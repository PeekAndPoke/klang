A simulation of the rules in the code, not a recording. A highlight is early by when its note sounds minus when the frontend's clock expected it. The first round is timed before the answer is back, for both frontends; the new frontend's later highlights are within 30 ms in the spec (KlangPlaybackSyncSpec), shown as 0. The delay of 200 ms is a scenario setting, made long so the effect shows; it is not measured. PlaybackClock reaches the backend's reports at once; the real report lags by a poll and the message, which the post names as open.

The model's settings and the rules it ports

The answer takes the same delay back, and no number depends on that. Blocks of 128 frames at 48 kHz; the backend reports every 8th block (21.3 ms); PlaybackClock is read every 16 ms. One note a second (60 rpm). Bursts: 36 blocks (96 ms) at once, every 96 ms. Rules at 14a4f8f18: PlaybackClock.kt, VoiceScheduler.ensureEpoch, PlaybackEngineDispatcher.emitDiagnostics, KlangPatternScheduler.begin.
