## 1. Why NOT "Motör Hits" (the decision that started this)

- The exposure was never **copyright** (names/single words aren't copyrightable) — it's **trademark**.
- **Motörhead** (The 2015 Kilmister Trust) holds a verified word mark (US Reg. 4802001) covering
  **earphones/headphones/phone cases → International Class 9**, the *same class as apps/software*, and the
  estate **actively licenses the name into digital music products** (NetEnt slot, Rock Band, Rocksmith).
- "Motör **Hits**": "Hits" is generic → the dominant term is **"Motör"**, which is also the dominant root of
  "**Motör**head." Shared dominant term + music sector + app store = real **likelihood-of-confusion** + the
  acute risk of an **app-store takedown** (Apple/Google pull apps fast on an IP complaint, no lawsuit needed).
  For a series whose whole purpose is to *finance the platform*, that downside is asymmetric.
- The **metal umlaut (ö)** hurts here — it's Motörhead's most distinctive visual signature; adopting it points
  a straight line at the senior mark.
- ✅ **Resolution:** consumer apps are **"Klang Hits"**; "Motör" stays as the behind-glass engine metaphor,
  where the collision risk is **much lower** (different lead word "Klang", Motör reads descriptively as
  *motor/engine*, different goods/lane). Still a with-counsel call, but sound.

## 3. The trademark to file

- **File:** **stylized `Klang.art`** (ideally a logo/wordmark version) — **not** bare "Klang."
    - Bare "Klang" is the ordinary German word for *sound* → **descriptive/weak** and **crowded** (KLANG:technologies,
      Klangio, KLANGLOBBY). You can't own it as a category namespace.
    - `Klang.art` as a **composite whole** is meaningfully stronger: per the *Booking.com* principle a TLD adds no
      *automatic* significance, but a weak word + TLD can form a protectable mark judged on consumer perception;
      ".art" is also *evocative* (art/creativity), which helps the overall impression.
- **Classes (Nice):** **9** (downloadable apps/software — core) + **41** (music education/entertainment — *we teach
  music*, so this genuinely fits). Optional later: **42** (SaaS / non-downloadable software).
- **Also file for equity where recognition accrues:** consider **"Klang Hits"** (wordmark and/or stylized) for the
  consumer series — that's where marketing spend and user recognition will build.

> ⚠️ **`Klang.art GmbH` (Handelsregister) ≠ trademark.** The company-name registration is a **separate registry**.
> It gives some protection against similar company names but does **not** register your brand in your goods/services
> classes. If you want both the entity *and* the brand right, you file **both**.

## 4. The one clearance check we won't skip

- **Klangio (klang.io)** is the nearest real conflict: German, same sector, **same Class 9 music software**.
- Our "we do the **opposite** (notation/code → audio; they do audio → notation)" argument is **strong on
  real-world confusion** but **weak at the classification level** — examiners compare goods/service *categories*,
  and "downloadable music software" vs. "downloadable music software" reads as *related* regardless of direction.
- The crowded "Klang" field means **coexistence is normal** (a shield) — but also that our own "Klang" mark is
  **weak** and can't exclude the next Klang startup (not a sword). Distinctiveness must come from the **full
  `Klang.art` form**, not the word.
- [ ] **Action:** one **clearance search / short Markenanwalt consult** on `Klang.art` in Class 9 + 41, specifically
  vs. **Klangio** and **KLANG:technologies**. *Highest-value spend in the whole plan.*

## 5. How registration works (first-timer walkthrough)

**Flow (same shape everywhere):**
clearance search → file (word vs. stylized; pick classes) → examination (descriptiveness objections land here) →
publication + **~3-month opposition window** → registration → renew every **10 years**.
**Timeline:** straightforward unopposed EU/DE marks ≈ **4–7 months**. DPMA offers accelerated exam for **+€200**.

**Official fees (web-verified 2026-07-09 — reconfirm before filing):**

| Route                   | Fee                                                                           | Coverage                                                |
|-------------------------|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------|
| 🇩🇪 **DPMA** (Germany) | **€290** electronic, up to 3 classes; +€100/class beyond 3                    | Germany only                                            |
| 🇪🇺 **EUIPO / EUTM**   | **€850** first class, **+€50** 2nd, **+€150** each further (2 classes = €900) | All 27 EU states, one filing                            |
| 🌍 **WIPO Madrid**      | **653 CHF** basic (B&W) + per-country fees                                    | ~130 countries — **needs a base DE/EU filing first**    |
| 🇺🇸 **USPTO**          | **$350 / class**                                                              | US; claim 6-month Paris priority back to EU filing date |

**Do you need a Markenanwalt?** DIY-able: the mechanical online filing, domains, obvious classes.
**Where counsel earns its fee:** (1) clearance search (esp. Klangio); (2) class wording (avoid too-narrow /
surcharges); (3) office actions (if examiner calls `Klang.art` descriptive); (4) oppositions (fights run
**€5k–15k**, so avoiding one cheaply is high-value).
**Attorney ballpark:** DE ≈ €500–1,000 + €290 official; EU ≈ €750–1,500 + €850+ official; standalone clearance ≈ €300+.

**💰 Budget lever — EUIPO SME Fund 2026:** reimburses **75%** of trademark fees for EU SMEs. *But* the 2026
trademark voucher appeared **exhausted** at last check (the pot goes fast). **Check the SME Fund page directly
before filing** — if a tranche is live it roughly quarters the fee cost. Don't count on it; don't miss it.

## 6. The sequence — before vs. after announcing

**🔴 BEFORE any big public announcement** (EU is **first-to-file** → announcing broadcasts an unregistered name to
squatters; your own disclosure doesn't bar *you*, but it invites *them*):

- [ ] Register the **GmbH** (`Klang.art GmbH`) — separate track, *not* the trademark
- [ ] Grab **Tier-1 domains/handles** (esp. `klanghits.com` while free)
- [ ] **Clearance consult** on `Klang.art` (Class 9+41) vs. Klangio + KLANG:technologies
- [ ] **Check the EUIPO SME Fund** for the 75% rebate
- [ ] **File one EUTM for stylized `Klang.art`**, Class **9 + 41** (~€900 official + ~€750–1,500 attorney)

**🟢 CAN WAIT (after announce):**

- [ ] **US filing** (USPTO, $350/class) within the **6-month Paris priority window**, backdated to the EU date —
  when the app-store push is real
- [ ] Class 42 (SaaS), defensive domain variants, secondary marks (e.g. "Klang Hits")
- [ ] **Skip Madrid / other countries** until traction justifies it
- [ ] Standalone `Motör` / `Der Motör` — **likely never file**; keep as the unregistered behind-glass engine metaphor

**Realistic all-in to be properly protected before launch:** ≈ **€1,700–2,500** (GmbH costs separate) —
potentially **~€900–1,400** if the SME Fund rebate is live.
