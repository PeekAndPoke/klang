## November and December: the plan

Decided in the Q4 planning with the maintainer (2026-10-28). Four threads.

### The Motor V1

V1 is the scope of `docs/tasks/_v1-scope.md`, without a fully configurable engine (that is V2; configurable side
chains are V1.5, both in the Q1 2027 plan). What still changes the shape a song writer types, or the sound:

- the pitch pipeline (KLANG-00245): the listening round and the composition block;
- cut groups first (KLANG-00167), then voice takeover (KLANG-00138, phase 1 blocked on its design) and glide (its
  phase 2);
- the `snd*` sound functions (KLANG-00101, KLANG-00189) with sound banks of Ignitor instruments (KLANG-00352), as one
  design round.

A later Motor version may change a sound; the maintainer accepts that trade-off "for staying in a high gear while
noone has written any music yet except us."

### Tutorials

- Fix the current set and build the through-line (KLANG-00131), along the curriculum (KLANG-00145) and the master
  plan (KLANG-00086).
- New: authoring instruments with the Ignitor (KLANG-00378).
- Tutorials on the workbench (KLANG-00370, in progress).

### Interactive learning tools (new)

- Sound puzzles: tutorials unlocked like a tech tree by solving them (KLANG-00377). The maintainer has ideas; the
  details come in a conversation.
- Close by: the ear trainer (KLANG-00274) and the first-visit trail (KLANG-00350, in progress).

### Finish the UI redesign

The UI workbench (KLANG-00334) to its end:

- the song page on the new parts (KLANG-00349, in progress), the editor's colours per look (KLANG-00368), the
  remaining pages moved onto the new parts;
- a theme editor, the "Custom" look (KLANG-00376);
- a key map editor (KLANG-00325 step 4: every key, record your own, clashes, back to the defaults).

### Not in Q4 (the maintainer, 2026-10-28)
