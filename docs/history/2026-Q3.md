# 2026 Q3: One shape for the engine (Jul to Sep 2026)

The plan for Q3 had two acts: finish the engine, then spend the rest of the quarter on tutorials.
The first act grew and took the quarter. After a quiet July (songs and a summer breather), August
brought the master stage, the phase pool, block-size parity and a long run of parameter and filter
unification; on 2026-08-31 we drew the V1 line (the engine and its surfaces stable before the
tutorials). September then gave the engine its present shape in the signal flow redesign: a
Katalyst chain on every orbit and at the master, every voice an Ignitor tree, the voice strip and
the Pipeline DSL retired. Tutorials restarted from scratch in August and then waited for the engine.
Alongside: the whitepaper, a blog series, and a lot of process. 803 commits, v0.1.2 to v0.4.5.

## 🔊 Audio engine

- **Body and vowel at the orbit** (July): moved from per-voice filters to orbit effects, KLANG-00128
  (body vowel orbit katalyst), with a material library, a floor and a crossfade, KLANG-00132 (body
  vowel materials floor crossfade). The live-update "double voice" fixed, KLANG-00133 (live update
  double voice fix); a `runSongBenchmark` harness and pipeline optimizations (PR #63).
- **Master-in-pattern** (2026-08-03), KLANG-00136 (master dsl): the master rides the voice stream,
  crossfades on swap and works offline; reverb and delay parameter parity on orbit and master buses.
  The limiter got **lookahead** (2026-08-11), KLANG-00225 (master limiter lookahead), and the tuning
  constants one home in `audio_bridge`, KLANG-00141 (audio bridge constants).
- **Offline = live**: 128 frames pinned everywhere, KLANG-00139 (block size parity), and sample voices
  no longer start up to a block late, KLANG-00140 (sample voice onset quantization).
- **Unison phase pool** (2026-08-12, v0.2.0), KLANG-00144 (unison phase pool): banded start phases
  end the super-oscillator "fundamental lottery" (onset holes 16 % to 0). Opt-in per song.
- **Block-framing invariance** (v0.3.5): a note sounds the same wherever it lands in a block, proven
  node by node; the scheduler's startup race (exactly one block) fixed as a clock convention.
- **Voices that end right**: realtime note-off, KLANG-00157 (realtime note off gate release); the
  double-ADSR cut tail, KLANG-00161 (ignitor envelope ownership); voice culling of inaudible
  releases, KLANG-00199 (voice culling).
- **Soundfonts loop**, KLANG-00171 (soundfont looping investigation) and KLANG-00172 (sample metadata
  lost on chunk path): three stacked defects meant no soundfont had ever looped in the browser.
- **Resource warehouse** (2026-09-04), KLANG-00230 (resource warehouse): delay rings, reverb networks
  and cylinders rented from one shelf, never allocated in render, plus a warmup vocabulary and a
  closed-form tail. The Fairphone's resource stutter is almost gone. KLANG-00176 (per playback engine)
  closed the same day with engine disposal.
- **Speed**: the unified EqCore, graph optimizer folds, KLANG-00202 (ignitor optimizer arithmetic
  folds); polynomial sines; DriftLanes, KLANG-00198 (drift lanes analog spread); sine partial banks.
- **By ear**: a stereo reverb, KLANG-00254 (stereo reverb); filter drift at twice the pitch drift,
  KLANG-00248 (analog drift ratio tuning).

## 🧬 The signal flow redesign (September)

- Designed 2026-09-17 with the maintainer, KLANG-00210 (signal flow redesign): three owners, one
  setter each. Phases 1 to 3 done by 2026-09-28.
- **Katalyst DSL**, KLANG-00236 (katalyst dsl), built in three days: every orbit gets a chain (reverb,
  delay, eq, body, vowel, compressor, duck, phaser), and every stage switches and changes without clicks.
- **Built-in instruments**, KLANG-00237 (builtin instruments): every voice is an Ignitor tree and
  `classic()` is the sprudel voice; the voice strip and the Pipeline DSL retired (v0.3.18).
- **The master is a Katalyst** at the output, KLANG-00238 (phase3 step12 master as katalyst); the
  Master DSL retired (v0.4.0). The tremolo became a composition of the oscillators (v0.4.1).
- A test consolidation after the redesign, KLANG-00242 (test consolidation).

## 🎛 DSLs: Ignitor and sprudel

- **Parameter and filter unification** (August), KLANG-00227 (filter unification): one default q,
  one wet/dry law, depths in semitones, exponential ADSR curves, 50 alias names deleted; a filter's
  frequency is `freq` everywhere, KLANG-00153 (filter frequency param naming); klangblocks removed.
- **Configure lambdas** on every sub-typed door, KLANG-00179 (dsl configure lambdas), and **field
  accessors**, KLANG-00184 (sprudel field accessors): every compound an object with named slots, the
  per-knob doors gone. Reverb and delay got one word each, KLANG-00208 (reverb naming unification)
  and KLANG-00209 (delay names and send defaults).
- Door shapes settled, one envelope law with six hosts, `min`/`max` as clamps, filter curves,
  KLANG-00215 (filter envelope configuration).
- sprudel: tweaks in mini-notation, KLANG-00162 (mini notation tweaks); continuous controls in
  arithmetic, KLANG-00195 (sprudel arithmetic continuous controls).
- Declined: KLANG-00147 (resonator swing), KLANG-00152 (fractional pitch input). The Motor got its
  one-word name (v0.3.0), and the umlaut was retired on 2026-08-25: Klangmotor.
