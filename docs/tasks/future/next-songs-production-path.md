# The next songs: three references that pull the missing production tools in

Status: **future, waits for the engine tidy-up** (`docs/tasks/engine-tidy-up.md`). Agreed with the maintainer
2026-10-08: "Let us wait until the current engine work / cleanup is done ... Will come back soon for writing the next
song with you."

## Why

The research in [`../../plans/aaa-production-tricks.md`](../../plans/aaa-production-tricks.md) found about 180
production techniques and mapped each onto the engine. The maintainer's rule for what comes next: "building the things
just to have them built will not help. We need actual songs to try these things." A technique is built when a song
falls short without it.

## The plan (section 10 of the research)

1. **Deep house, 120 BPM**, the reference Imany, "Don't Be So Shy" (Filatov & Karas remix). Proves the duck with a
   band, the width stage, the reverb's pre-delay, feed filter and self-duck, the delay's feedback filter, the shelves,
   the master clipper.
2. **Synth-pop, around 1986 to 1990**, Depeche Mode. Proves the bus chorus, a gated snare reverb, metal percussion from
   inharmonic partials, a hard-sync lead.
3. **EBM**: Nitzer Ebb, DAF, Front 242. Proves hard compression, the transient core, short rooms.

## The first step

The listening sheet for the reference: the maintainer listens with Claude's questions in hand. The questions cover
the sections, the elements and their bands, what pumps, what is wide, and where the room is. Then comes a first draft
with today's engine. Section 10.3 of the research has the loop. The song is original: the reference's tempo, palette
and production, never its melody, hook, chords or lyrics.
