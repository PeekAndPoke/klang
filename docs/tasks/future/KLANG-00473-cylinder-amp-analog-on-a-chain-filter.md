# KLANG-00473: Cylinder amp: analog on a chain filter

> **Status (2026-11-18): IDEA.** From the maintainer's rework of KLANG-00466 (2026-11-18): 'we should only offer analog I guess?'. The Ignitor's filter lambda offers analog (the state-dependent damping, 0 to 10); the chain filters of KLANG-00467 are EQ sections, which cannot run it. This needs a filter stage of its own on the chain (the voice's analog filter at the bus position, left and right), with analog on the lambda of lowpass and highpass, and a lambda on bandpass and notch. No guitar of Der Schmetterling writes analog on a filter; A Truth Worth Lying For and Sakura do, on voices. Part of KLANG-00466. After step 1.
