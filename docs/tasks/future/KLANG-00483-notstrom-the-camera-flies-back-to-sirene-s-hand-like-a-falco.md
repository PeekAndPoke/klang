# KLANG-00483: notstrom the camera flies back to Sirene's hand like a falcon

> **Status (2026-11-24): IDEA.** The maintainer's idea, 2026-11-24; not before the token refill on Friday.

## Why this exists

The maintainer (2026-11-24): "We have the camera move back to FOH when Sirene approaches the camera. Then she could
walk around back to the front position or any position. Extend her left arm and the camera will fly in until it sits
on her hand like a falcon returning to his master."

Today the first half exists: when Sirene comes walking straight at the camera while it is on the total, the camera
takes off backward over the audience, stays at the mixing desk a while and flies back in to the total by itself
(`Choreography.retreat`, `Approaching`, KLANG-00478). The idea is the second half: she calls it back.

## The idea, as said

1. The camera is out at the mixing desk after her approach, as now.
2. She walks back to the front, or to any place on the stage.
3. She extends her left arm.
4. The camera flies in and lands on her hand, like a falcon on its master's glove.

## Decide before implementing

Nothing is designed yet. Questions that come up at first sight, for the maintainer:

- What the picture is once the camera sits on her hand: a close look at her from her own hand, or a look out from
  the hand over the stage or the crowd.
- How it leaves the hand again, and when: she lets it fly, or the next round takes it.
- Whether it happens every time the camera went out before her, or now and then.
- Der Schmetterling only (she strolls there with the trumpet in her right hand), or also where she has a guitar.
- The arm is a move of hers: by the direction of KLANG-00480 it would be a skill on her sheet, and the camera's
  part a move of the choreography that waits for it.
