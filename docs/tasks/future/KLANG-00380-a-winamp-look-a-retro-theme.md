> **Status (2026-10-28): IDEA.** an idea filed away, not now
>
> **Part of:** KLANG-00334 (UI workbench: the Motor-centred layout, tool windows and the first-visit trail)

## Why this exists

The maintainer, 2026-10-28, looking at Winamp's classic skin for its spectrum's peak cells: "Also a winamp theme would
be totally retro... not now just an idea to file away".

What the classic skin has that a look could borrow: bevelled grey-blue metal and dark slate grounds, an LCD in green on
black for the time and the title (`LcdInk`), a spectrum in green to yellow to orange with grey peak cells, chunky
bevelled buttons, a pixel font for the readouts. A look sets colours and amounts only (`KlangRole`); the bevels and the
pixel font would need more than a look can say today.
