# KLANG-00332: UX comfort: the everyday helpers of the editor

> **Status (2026-10-25): IDEA.** An epic: its progress is its children's statuses (`ticket list --epic KLANG-00332
> --deep`).

## Why this exists

The maintainer (2026-10-25) named the epic "UX comfort": the small helpers that make working in the editor
comfortable, finding your way around a song, getting back to where you were, reaching an action without the mouse.
Each one is small; together they decide how the editor feels in daily use.

## What belongs here

Helpers for moving around and acting in the editor: navigation, jumping, the keyboard, the clipboard, the editor's
overview of a song. Not here: the editor's diagnostics (KLANG-00251, its own umbrella), the editor's intelligence
(completion, types: KLANG-00064, KLANG-00217), the look (KLANG-00207).
