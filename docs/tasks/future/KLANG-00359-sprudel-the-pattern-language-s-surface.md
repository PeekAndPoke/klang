# KLANG-00359: Sprudel: the pattern language's surface

> **Status (2026-10-27): IDEA.** An epic: its progress is its children's statuses (`ticket list --epic KLANG-00359
> --deep`).

## Why this exists

Sprudel is how most songs are written. Its surface should be small, consistent and pleasant: the sound doors, mini-notation, the readers of a compound, parity with Kotlin, and tests that cover it.

## What belongs here

Sprudel's functions and doors as a song writer sees them: the snd* family, sound banks (KLANG-00352), mini-notation, slot readers, solo, Kotlin parity, the module's test coverage. Not here: the KlangScript language itself (KLANG-00360), what the editor knows about it (KLANG-00361), the engine's wiring (KLANG-00354).
