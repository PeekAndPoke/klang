## Why this exists

What makes a sound in the first place: Ignitor instruments and their parts, new oscillators, chip-style and sung voices, samples and soundfonts. A song can only be as good as the sources it can reach for.

## What belongs here

Instruments (Ignitor), oscillators, physical and modal models, curves as nodes, FM, the doors of an instrument and velocity, samples, soundfonts and the sample mirror. Not here: effects after the source (KLANG-00357), how sprudel names and picks a sound (KLANG-00359).
