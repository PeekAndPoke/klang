## Why this exists

The editor should know the code as well as the writer does: complete what can come next, know the type of a stored lambda, and point at a mistake (a typo in a shape, a runtime error, a doubtful voice door) where it is.

## What belongs here

IntelliSense, type inference in the editor, diagnostics (KLANG-00251 is their umbrella, with its own children). Not here: the editor's comfort helpers and keys (KLANG-00332), the language itself (KLANG-00360).
