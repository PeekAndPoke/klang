## Why this exists

KlangScript is the language every song is written in. What it can express (operators on native objects, functions as values, union types) and how its docs read decide how far a song writer can go.

## What belongs here

The language: syntax, semantics, the interpreter, native interop, types as the language knows them, the language's docs. Not here: what the editor makes of it (KLANG-00361), sprudel's surface (KLANG-00359).
